Algo Trade Simulator

Pre-trade cost analysis: slippage, fees, market impact

Prabhat Kumar · solo · IIT Kharagpur

Does not place trades. Shows what a market order would cost.

The problem

What people see

  • Mid price
  • Fill assumed at mid
  • Clean backtest PnL

What they pay

  • Slippage
  • Fees
  • Market impact

Why it hurts

  • Thin book kills a small edge
  • Live looks worse than paper
  • Strategy vs cost is mixed

Who it is for

  • Students and first algo writers — not a broker
  • No exchange API keys. Nothing is sent to a venue
  • Change size, side, fee tier in the browser
  • Synthetic book if Redis or a live feed is down

What I built

A Dash dashboard on a live or synthetic L2 book. Estimates slippage, fees, Almgren–Chriss impact, maker/taker, net cost.

FeedManager picks the source. TradeSimulator is the only place the models run.

Dashboard

One run — BTC-USDT buy $100

  • Slippage $0.0062 (0.0062%)
  • Fees $0.1201 (0.1201%)
  • Impact $0.0055 (0.0055%)
  • Net $0.1319 (0.1319%) · latency 0.75 ms

Fees dominate a small order. Impact stays tiny at $100.
